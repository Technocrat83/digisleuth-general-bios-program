# Separation of Powers

`Declaration != Authentication != Admission != Execution != Diagnosis`

Science determines what has been demonstrated. To Market determines what can legitimately be commercially translated. Authentication determines what external evidence supports. Admission determines whether a specimen shape is admissible. Execution authorization determines whether action is currently permitted. Diagnosis determines what may be observed/classified within scope.
