# Spectral Goalpost Reconstructive Helm Petition Protocol v0.1

## Founder correction

External control surfaces may petition the sealed Spectral Goalpost battery for a bounded reconstruction of historical Jurisdictional Helm state.

`Control-Surface Petition != Battery Access != Reconstruction Admission != Helm Authority`

The battery is a sealed evidence substrate. It does not accept direct operator queries, disclose hidden fixtures, admit petitions, or instantiate a live Helm state.

## Reconstruction chain

`Control Surface -> Authenticated Petition -> JLK Localization -> External Admission -> Read-Only Goalpost Replay -> Candidate Reconstructed Helm State -> Conformance Witness -> Return`

JLK localizes the requested temporal, causal, spectral, and jurisdictional cone. JLK does not admit the petition.

An admitted replay emits:

`H_hat_JR(k) = <I_E, I_R, TC_k, H_k, S_k, P_k, U_k, J_k, M_R, L_R>`

where:

- `I_E`: sealed BIOS epoch identity;
- `I_R`: recording/session identity;
- `TC_k`: complete SMPTE-address tuple including rate and DF/NDF mode;
- `H_k`: authenticated goalpost seal;
- `S_k`: reconstructible spectral state;
- `P_k`: predicate/standing state recorded at the goalpost;
- `U_k`: unresolved coordinates preserved without completion;
- `J_k`: historical jurisdictional localization;
- `M_R`: reconstruction-method identity and parameters;
- `L_R`: replay and derivation lineage.

`H_hat_JR(k) != H_J(current) != HelmAuthority`

A reconstruction is a typed historical candidate. It cannot silently become current state, grant authority, repair absent residue, or execute a transition.

## Petition contract

Every petition must bind:

- petitioner and control-surface identity;
- requested epoch and SMPTE-address range;
- first and terminal goalpost hashes;
- requested plane coordinates;
- reconstruction purpose and method;
- declared authority ceiling;
- return channel;
- petition hash and signature/authentication witness.

The only admissible decision states are:

`ADMITTED_FOR_READ_ONLY_REPLAY | DENIED | UNRESOLVED | OUT_OF_JURISDICTION`

The battery never assigns these states to itself.

## Cross-surface request rules

A requesting surface may:

- petition for an authenticated historical reconstruction;
- declare the planes and interval it needs;
- receive typed reconstructed residue and uncertainty;
- compare a reconstruction with its present observation.

A requesting surface may not:

- address hidden fixtures directly;
- select or adapt fixture order;
- obtain expected verdicts or adjudicator feedback;
- rewrite a spectral goalpost;
- infer absent frames or missing spectral windows;
- treat reconstructed state as current Helm state;
- inherit Helm, admission, execution, or topology authority.
