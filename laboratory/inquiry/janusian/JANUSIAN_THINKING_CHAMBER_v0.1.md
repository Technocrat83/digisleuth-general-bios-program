## Purpose

Provide a bounded space for conceiving opposing ideas simultaneously, preserving the strongest intelligible form of each, and investigating what their tension reveals about assumptions, context, and possible constructions.

**Opposition may generate a question. It does not establish an answer.**

The term draws on the Janusian process associated with Albert Rothenberg: simultaneously conceiving and using opposite or antithetical ideas. This chamber is a proposed Digisleuth adaptation, not a validated implementation of his research. Terminology reference: [Albert Rothenberg — research overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Rothenberg).

## Inherited boundary

The parent charter distinguishes experience, interpretation, mechanism, ontology, and evidence. That separation governs every chamber record. Emotional intensity, symbolic resonance, novelty, and apparent elegance cannot increase a proposition’s evidentiary standing.

The chamber extends the existing node. Its creation does not instantiate a separate sovereign BIOS or authorize interventions, external execution, or constitutional change.

## Working grammar

**Orient → Pair → Co-hold → Differentiate → Generate → Challenge → Witness**

| Phase | Required work | Recorded output |
|---|---|---|
| Orient | Define the focal question, purpose, scope, and known evidence. | Bounded inquiry |
| Pair | Express two or more opposing propositions in their strongest defensible form. | Typed proposition set |
| Co-hold | Keep each proposition available for exploration without declaring either true. | Concurrent comparison record |
| Differentiate | Identify premises, definitions, scale, time, and jurisdiction behind the opposition. | Tension classification |
| Generate | Derive possible reframings, constructions, or hypotheses while preserving the original propositions. | Candidate set, possibly empty |
| Challenge | Identify contrary observations, alternative explanations, and a discriminating test where applicable. | Challenge record |
| Witness | Preserve sources, changes, uncertainty, and disposition. | Session receipt |

“Co-hold” is an instruction for structured consideration. It is not a claim about an AI system’s consciousness or internal computational simultaneity.

## Tension classes

- **Logical contradiction:** propositions conflict under the same definitions and conditions. Preserve the conflict; do not infer arbitrary conclusions from it.
- **Contextual opposition:** different times, scales, roles, or jurisdictions explain the apparent contradiction.
- **Value tension:** legitimate objectives compete and require explicit tradeoffs.
- **Interpretive plurality:** several meanings fit a report; meaning alone does not select a causal explanation.
- **Empirical rivalry:** explanations make different observable predictions and require evidence.

Opposing ideas need not carry equal evidentiary weight. A synthesis is optional; rejection, unresolved tension, and abstention are valid outcomes.

## Illustrative entry — not an executed session

**Question:** How can an experience be personally meaningful while its proposed external cause remains unestablished?

**Face A:** Preserve the person’s experience and its significance.

**Face B:** Require independent evidence for claims about external causation.

**Tension diagnosis:** These concerns occupy different claim domains and can coexist.

**Candidate construction:** Maintain separate records for reported experience, personal meaning, and causal hypotheses. Link them without transferring evidentiary standing between them.

This illustrates the parent rule: phenomenological respect does not entail epistemic endorsement.

## Cease and custody

CEASE is available throughout. On a halt, stop forward exploration and preserve available residue, the reason, and unresolved questions. Human-facing work inherits the parent charter’s consent, withdrawal, privacy, and safety requirements; the chamber does not diagnose, induce altered states, or turn metaphysical interpretations into established facts.
