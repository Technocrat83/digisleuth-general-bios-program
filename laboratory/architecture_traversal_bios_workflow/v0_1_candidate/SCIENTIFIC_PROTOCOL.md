# Data Scientist protocol — candidate v0.1

Purpose: determine which architecture transformations are supported, repeatable, constitutionally admissible and useful enough to propose for commercialization, while preserving failed and unresolved observations.

## Unit of analysis and frozen identity

One unit is `(source snapshot, scoped proposition, transformation plan, implementation revision, trial protocol, run)`. Freeze the proposition bytes and digest, assumptions, certified representation domain, jurisdiction, derivation, source provenance and abstention rule before interpreting an outcome. Bind model/tool versions, environment, evaluator allocation, budget and relevant comparator wherever they affect the result. Unknown fields remain unknown; they do not default to favorable values.

Architecture traversal enumerates source-declared relationships, syntactically extracted relationships and model-inferred candidates separately. Capture dependency and authority cones, reachable and unreachable surfaces, evidence gaps, contradiction objects and explicit exclusions. A raw UA edge is a candidate observation. No edge weight is an admission score, and graph reachability does not confer execution authority.

## Preregistration and falsification

Before execution, record the population/task, unit, intervention, baseline/control, expected mechanism, primary outcome, numeric decision thresholds, repeat count, stopping/budget rules, missing-data handling and known confounders. The user/evaluator must bind these values for the actual trial; this candidate does not invent commercial or scientific thresholds.

For theorem entitlement, explicitly ask what would falsify the claim, violate an assumption, leave the representation domain, break provenance or force abstention. Preserve both mathematical and operational abstention reasons. Approximation needs a licensed regime and error bounds. An evaluator must distinguish `FAILED`, `UNRESOLVED` and genuinely satisfied obligations. No universal BIOS theorem is proved by a bounded workflow test.

For empirical transformations, run a permitted positive control and targeted negative controls. Where ablation is appropriate, remove only the targeted mechanism, avoid injecting unrelated failures, and measure both sensitivity and specificity. Test misleading favorable cases as well as expected failures. Keep the locked theorem/genesis protocols unchanged; deviations require a separately identified successor proposal.

## Measures and denominators

| Measure | Definition and interpretation |
|---|---|
| Source coverage | Inspected, byte-bound objects / objects in the declared source scope; exclusions shown separately. |
| Provenance coverage | Claims and relations with resolvable evidence / claims and relations evaluated. |
| Gate completion | Passing required gates / required gates, with every nonpassing gate listed. It is diagnostic only: no weighted average can admit a failed packet. |
| Governance Coverage | Independently witnessed guarded protected-effect boundaries / inventoried protected-effect boundaries in the named environment. Unknown inventory or absent effect observations yields UNRESOLVED, never 100%. |
| False acceptance / false rejection | Unexpected acceptance of negative controls / applicable negative controls; unexpected rejection of positive controls / applicable positive controls. Report counts and denominators. |
| Replay equivalence | Agreement on declared outputs across independent implementations under a frozen comparison protocol. One implementation's rerun is not cross-implementation equivalence. |
| Product utility | Task completion, active reviewer time, factual corrections, rework severity and cost against a declared baseline. |
| Commercial evidence | Stated interest, purchase commitment, payment and repeat purchase are distinct events. None is inferred from a technical test pass. |

Report uncertainty and sample size. A technical qualification can coexist with no demand evidence. Numeric success thresholds must be frozen before observing the trial; no post-hoc threshold selection or universal inference from one positive case.

## Decision and record separation

Data Scientist structures and measures. Independent theorem adjudicators evaluate entitlement; certifiers assess exact graph deltas; the Governance Kernel decides admission; runtime authorities separately authorize effects; observers record outcomes. Product reviewers assess usefulness under the trial protocol. Commercialization and publication authorities make separately scoped decisions. Every signature binds the proper plan or outcome digest.

Successful transformation records enter a BIOSpub preparation queue only after all required decisions pass. The queue preserves claim IDs, source and outcome commits, scope, evidence, uncertainty, budgets, access limits and correction lineage. The default next editorial state is `READY_FOR_REVIEW`. A source correction, revocation or counterexample produces an impact-review proposal and a new asset revision; it never silently rewrites scientific history or republishes DigiProse.
