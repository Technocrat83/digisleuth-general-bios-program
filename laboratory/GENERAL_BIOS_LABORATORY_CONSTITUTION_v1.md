# GENERAL_BIOS_LABORATORY_CONSTITUTION_v1

**Status:** RATIFIED  
**Scope:** Experimental infrastructure only

## Constitutional Law

The Laboratory may instantiate, observe, preserve, hash, serialize, execute, and return.

The Laboratory may not interpret, adjudicate, repair scientific deficiencies, mutate frozen protocols, infer missing referents, promote hypotheses, or manufacture historical provenance.

## Separation of Powers

- Recovery discovers or authenticates existing referents.
- Genesis creates new prospective referents only under explicit authorization.
- Binding authenticates material execution identities.
- Execution produces runtime residue.
- Ingress verifies physical evidence.
- Adjudication determines scientific standing.
- Promotion remains separate.

## Closure

No downstream stage may repair an upstream deficiency.

Deficiency Detection != Deficiency Resolution Authority.

## Anti-Substitution

Related Artifact != Bound Experimental Referent.

No prior runner, fixture, theorem organism, coupling implementation, or phenotype evaluator may be coerced into an experiment merely because it is semantically related.

## Evidence Law

No local bytes, no local evidence.  
No local evidence, no adjudication.  
No adjudication, no scientific standing delta.

## Provenance Law

Recomputation can verify a historical commitment; it cannot create one.

Prospective genesis != historical recovery.
