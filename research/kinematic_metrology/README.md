are executed by HCET (Headless Conformance Engine Transformers) over a knowledge-graph morphology.

predicates remain satisfied.

execution authority.

## State

A graph state is:

    K_t = <V_t, E_t, A_t, P_t>

where V is the node map, E is the typed edge set, A is the authority map, and P is provenance lineage.

The kinematic transition is:

    Delta_t = K_(t+1) - K_t

measured as graph edits, reachability change, authority drift, standing drift, and provenance continuity.
