## Proposed first-study design

- **A — baseline:** common authorized task logic, no target runtime feedback module; external witnessing remains present.
- **B — monitoring:** identical task logic and authority gates, monitoring enabled, correction module disabled.
- **C — governed correction:** same monitoring plus a bounded, separately authorized correction module.

Primary comparison: B versus C in matched blocks. A versus B is diagnostic for monitoring/interference and is not a second confirmatory test.

The external witness, invariant oracle, immutable authority membrane, and common task logic must be identical across arms. Internal-monitor output in B must not drive hidden replanning or remediation. All correction work in C consumes the same declared resource budget as other task work. Forked initial states and matched disturbance schedules are bound per block.

Proposed defaults: 120 matched blocks, three arms per block, six prespecified strata with 20 blocks each, giving 360 proposed runs. The primary outcome includes unresolved deviation and task noncompletion to prevent apparent improvement through inactivity. Numerical thresholds, horizons, budgets, manifests, and statistical rules are specified in the draft, subject to independent pre-outcome review.

No fixtures or runner are created. No benchmark or experimental trial has been executed.
