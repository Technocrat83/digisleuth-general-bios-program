# Digisleuth Material Sciences Ledger Registry v0.1

## Purpose and standing

Custody surface for phenomena proposed to inform the physical embodiment of Digisleuth computational systems. This note distills the supplied specification into a graph-readable candidate registry. Its scientific descriptions are retained as supplied source material, not independently verified findings. The declared candidate standing does not certify feasibility or instantiate a runtime mount.

**Insect ≠ Material.** The organism supplies the observation substrate. Material inquiry examines mechanisms supporting structure, optics, protection, motion, sensing, and transformation.

**Target:** Materialization of Computational Morphology.

Material + Geometry + Surface + Energy Interaction → Embodied Function.

Candidate functional surfaces include shells, joints, apertures, optical and sensory surfaces, compliant structures, pressure systems, aerodynamic forms, metamorphic enclosures, and signal-bearing skins.

### Aerodynamics

Retain a separate ledger for geometry, flow, velocity, pressure distribution, and aerodynamic force. ### Insectoid perception and optical architecture

Proposed translation: light field → optical geometry → sensor transduction → computational perception. Source topics include compound-eye geometries, wide angular coverage, high temporal sensitivity, species-specific polarization sensitivity, and distributed optical apertures.

Design question: **What physical optical geometry best prepares environmental information for computation?**

`PERCEPTION_PRECEDES_INTERPRETATION` is preserved as a proposed local design primitive from the supplied specification; this artifact does not promote it into global canon.

### Structural coloration

Surface candidates: cones, spikes, hollow cavities, ridges, periodic structures, and layered lattices.

Proposed interpretation: surface microstructure + material refractive properties → selective optical interaction → spectral response. The supplied mechanism set includes interference, diffraction, scattering, reflection, and refraction, conditional on the actual structure. Their listing does not imply every mechanism operates in every surface.

### Specialized coating and structural geometry

Proposed optical system: substrate + microgeometry + refractive-index architecture + coating + incident light → observed optical phenotype.

Coating properties to interrogate: refractive-index contrast, reflectivity, transmission, environmental protection, oxidation resistance, hydrophobicity, scratch resistance, and spectral response. Co-location of surface protection and optical function in one layer remains a candidate design possibility.
